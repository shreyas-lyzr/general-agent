- **End-to-End Agentic Platform**  
  From visual design in Lyzr Studio to full REST-API integration, Lyzr empowers both citizen developers and engineers to build, test, and deploy intelligent agents without siloed toolchains.

- **Model-Agnostic Architecture**  
  Plug in any leading LLM—OpenAI GPT, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Amazon Bedrock,Perplexity—and switch between them at runtime to optimize for performance, cost, or domain expertise.

- **Built-In Responsible AI Controls**  
  Every agent automatically benefits from Lyzr’s Safe & Responsible AI features—bias mitigation, toxicity filtering, privacy enforcement, and detailed audit logs—ensuring compliance across industries (e.g., finance, healthcare, legal).

- **Modular RAG Pipelines**  
  Pre-configured retrieval-augmented generation workflows support over 600 pipeline permutations. Customize embeddings, vector stores, rerankers, and parsers without writing glue code.

- **Enterprise-Grade Governance & Security**  
  Role-based access control, SSO/SAML integration, encryption at rest and in transit, plus regional hosting options (HIPAA/SOC2 compliant) give global organizations peace of mind.

- **Extensive App Ecosystem**  
  Hundreds of plug-and-play templates and tool integrations (Slack, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Google Workspace, etc.) accelerate time-to-value and reduce implementation risk.
