## Introducing Lyzr: The Enterprise Agent Framework

Lyzr is a sophisticated agent framework designed with an **agentic approach** to building generative AI applications. By combining highly configurable agents with pre-built RAG pipelines and robust safety features, Lyzr enables developers to deploy enterprise-ready solutions in minutes rather than weeks.

### Flexible Deployment

Whether you prefer a low-code environment or direct integration, Lyzr fits your workflow:

* **Lyzr Studio:** Build, test, and iterate on your agents in a dedicated sandbox.
* **REST APIs:** Seamlessly integrate agentic capabilities directly into your existing tech stack via [comprehensive API documentation](https://agent-prod.studio.lyzr.ai/docs).

### Core Capabilities

Lyzr simplifies the complexity of modern AI, allowing you to launch high-impact tools with minimal effort:

* **Intelligent Search:** Advanced knowledge search and RAG-powered applications.
* **Data & Interaction:** AI chatbots and automated data-analysis assistants.
* **Workflow Automation:** Complex, multi-agent orchestrations for end-to-end business processes.
* **Safety First:** Built-in responsible AI guardrails to ensure secure enterprise adoption.

## Orchestration Modes

Lyzr provides two distinct orchestration paradigms:

- **Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) Orchestration**  
  Ideal when your workflow has well-defined, dependent tasks.  
  - Ensures all dependencies are resolved in order  
  - Provides full traceability  

- **Managerial Orchestration**  
  Suited for dynamic workflows where tasks evolve at runtime.  
  - A “manager” agent decomposes goals into subtasks  
  - Handles retries, branching, and fault recovery  
  - Adapts to changing requirements on the fly
