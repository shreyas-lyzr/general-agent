## What is Responsible AI, and Why Does It Matter?

Responsible AI ensures fairness, transparency, and security in AI systems by preventing bias, protecting sensitive data, and enabling ethical decision-making. This is critical for enterprises that rely on AI to automate business processes and make important decisions.
