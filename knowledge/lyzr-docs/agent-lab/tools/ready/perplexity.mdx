Lyzr integrates with **Perplexity AI** to enable real-time, factual search and web-grounded answer generation. Agents become smarter by querying Perplexity when knowledge bases are outdated or insufficient.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- Ask questions and get real-time, sourced answers.
- Automatically cite references and web links.
- Combine with RAG for fallback search.
- Ideal for time-sensitive or open-domain queries.

---

## 💼 Example Use Cases

### 1. Web-Sourced Assistant
When a question falls outside your KB, fallback to Perplexity for accurate and cited answers.

> **Prompt**: “What is the latest version of the Transformers library?”

### 2. Research Companion
Aggregate sources from Perplexity into a Notion page or document.

> **Workflow**: Question → Perplexity Answer → Markdown Export → Notion

### 3. Real-time Fact Checker
Use during meetings or document creation to verify claims from current news or science.

---

## 🧠 Smart Search Modes

- Simple (1-turn)
- Multi-hop (multi-turn refinement)
- Cite + compress answers for Slack or email summaries

---

## 🛠 How to Set Up

1. Go to **Tools > Add Perplexity AI Integration**.
2. Provide your Perplexity API key (if available).
3. Test with:
   > “What were the main announcements at WWDC 2025?”

---

Perplexity AI + Lyzr = always accurate, always grounded responses at your fingertips.
