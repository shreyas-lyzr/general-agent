With **Spotify** integration, Lyzr enables your AI agents to control music playback, manage playlists, and interact with users’ listening preferences—perfect for entertainment, mood tracking, and contextual personalization.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- Play, pause, skip, or resume tracks.
- Access user playlists and liked songs.
- Search for songs, artists, and genres.
- Create or update playlists dynamically.

---

## 💼 Example Use Cases

### 1. Mood-based Playlist Generator
Create or curate playlists based on sentiment detected from conversation.

> **Prompt**: “I’m feeling low today.”  
> **Agent**: “Here’s a calming playlist to lift your mood 🎵”

### 2. Context-aware DJ Bot
An AI DJ that picks songs based on time of day, weather, or your work mode.

> **Workflow**: Lyzr Agent → Weather/Time API → Spotify playlist update

### 3. Party Mode Playlist Agent
Let users add songs to a shared playlist using natural commands in a group chat.

> “Add ‘Levitating by Dua Lipa’ to my weekend party playlist.”

---

## 🔑 Permissions Required

- User-read-private
- User-library-read
- Streaming & Playback controls
- Playlist-modify-public/private

---

## 🛠 How to Set Up

1. Navigate to **Tools > Add Spotify Integration**.
2. Authenticate with your Spotify account (OAuth flow).
3. Grant read/write access to playlists and playback.
4. Test with:
   > “Play my morning productivity playlist now.”
